Chapter 01 Movement I — The Earth Aug 18

You Photographed Dinner Before You Ate It

Before it is a meal, it is a layout. We plate for the crop, season for the thumbnail, garnish for the scroll.

Listen — opening Recorded for Chapter 01 — arriving with the first dispatch.

This chapter traces how the camera got a seat at the table, and who is really being fed. The plate was always a frame. Now the frame has a feed, and the feed has a memory — it knows what performed last Tuesday, and it will ask, gently, for something like it again.

None of which makes the meal less real. The tomato is still a tomato. What changed is who else is at the table when you cut into it.
